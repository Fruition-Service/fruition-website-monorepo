# Fruition Services licensing and procurement

Buy your licences from the team that runs them.

Fruition is a certified partner for monday.com, Atlassian and HubSpot. We quote, buy and renew licences on behalf of clients: the right products, the right plan, a seat count matched to real usage, and every vendor incentive the client qualifies for, billed in AUD, GBP or USD.

Source page: https://www.fruitionservices.io/pricing

## How procurement works

Three rules we never break.

- Vendor pricing, no procurement fee. Licences are quoted at the vendor's own pricing. Partner compensation comes from the vendor, so nothing is added to the client's invoice for buying through us.
- Right-sized before you sign. monday.com sells seats in buckets: a minimum of 3, then multiples of 5. A team of six pays for ten unless someone checks. Products, plans and seats are sized against how the teams actually work before anything is quoted.
- One renewal calendar. One quote, one purchase order, one invoice across every vendor, with renewal dates aligned and a seat audit before each one.

## What we procure

- monday.com licences. All four products (work platform, CRM, dev and service) from Basic through Enterprise. Bundled products can sit on different plans but share one billing cycle, yearly billing lands 18% under monthly, and Enterprise carries a 12-month minimum term with every bundled product on Enterprise.

## Licensing FAQs

### Does buying licences through Fruition cost more than buying direct?

No. Licences are quoted at the vendor's own pricing, and partner compensation comes from the vendor rather than a margin added to the client's invoice. What changes is what the client ends up buying: the right products, the right plan tier, and a seat count matched to real usage.

### Which vendors can Fruition transact?

monday.com, where Fruition is a Platinum Partner, plus Atlassian and HubSpot. Fruition also advises on the AI and automation platforms it implements, and will say plainly when buying direct from the vendor is the better option.

### How does monday.com licensing actually price?

Per product and per seat bucket. There are four products (work platform, CRM, dev and service), each priced on its own plan ladder. Seats are sold in buckets starting at 3 and then rising in multiples of 5, so a team of six is billed for ten. Yearly billing is 18% cheaper than monthly. Products can be bundled on different plans but must share one billing cycle, and Enterprise requires every bundled product to sit on Enterprise with a minimum 12-month term.

### Can a seat count be reduced at renewal?

Yes, but the order matters. monday.com greys out any seat tier below the current active user count, so dormant users have to be deactivated before a smaller plan can be selected. A renewal review does that first and quotes second.

### What discounts is an organisation likely to qualify for?

Annual rather than monthly billing is the dependable one: 18% on monday.com, and lower effective rates on Atlassian Cloud. Beyond that, Atlassian publishes discounted Social Impact (nonprofit), Academic and Open Source licensing, and pays incentives for moving off Data Center, including dual licensing that keeps Data Center running alongside Cloud for up to a year at a 100% discount. Eligibility is verified by the vendor; Fruition prepares and lodges the application.

### Does HubSpot still charge an onboarding fee?

HubSpot requires onboarding on Professional and Enterprise purchases, but a certified Solutions Partner can have that fee waived and deliver the onboarding instead. HubSpot values the waiver at up to USD $6,000 per client.

### Does a client have to buy implementation services to buy licences?

No. Licensing and implementation are quoted separately and licences can be taken on their own, including as partner of record for a platform someone else built.

### How much does a Fruition implementation cost?

Implementation is fixed fee per phase with a named deliverable. monday.com implementations start around AUD $8,000 for a focused single-team build and run past $150,000 for enterprise multi-department deployments. The AI Capability Assessment is a fixed AUD $24,500 / GBP £14,500 / USD $18,500. Atlassian and HubSpot implementations and migrations start at AUD $12,000, training from AUD $2,500 per workshop, and managed services from AUD $3,000 a month. Every figure is confirmed in a written quote after a scoping call.

### Which currencies and entities can Fruition invoice in?

AUD, GBP and USD, from the Australian, UK and US entities. Multi-entity groups can be billed centrally or per region, and renewals can be co-terminated so one date covers every vendor.
